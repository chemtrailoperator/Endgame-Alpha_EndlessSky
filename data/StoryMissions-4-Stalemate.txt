#Investigating Alpha Base on Sagarra
mission "Endgame Alpha 4.0"
	landing
	source Sagarra
	name "Return to Vindemiatrix"
	description "Return to <destination> in <system> with the information you have collected regarding the Alphas."
	destination "New Argentina"
	to offer
		has "Endgame Alpha 3.14: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`After slipping through the fleets of patrolling Alpha warships, you have finally made it to the homebase of the Alphas.`
			`	The landscape is eerily similar to their last hideout, even down to the large reptiles roaming the earth.`
			`	Unlike that planet, this one is covered in a sprawl of factories, docks, and other industrializations to produce ships. The city was obviously planned meticulously, with major thoroughfares running radially out from a large tower in the distance.`
			`	A surface-to-air missle battery on the ground suddenly rotates up and fires at you. You shove the engines into maximum thrust and spin out of the way of the missile, thinking how you could have been spotted. You suddenly realize, shamefacedly, that you forgot to deactivate your atmospheric transponder. With that switched off, the surface system apparently seems to stop caring about you and stands down.`
			choice
				`	(Investigate Further)`
					goto factories
				`	(Leave. You've collected enough information.)`
					goto leave
			label factories
			`	You fly low over the buildings, looking for something more out of the ordinary than everything else. Eventually, you reach a section of shipyards that has enourmous hangars, and massive supply lines running into them.`
			choice
				`	(Continue collecting information.)`
				`	(Abort and leave.)`
					goto leave
			`	You hover your ship at a moderate distance from the shipyard and inspect it with a pair of binoculars you keep on hand. They are obviously building ships, but it is difficult to determine what kind they. You remember the massive supercarriers you fought in Men, and shiver at the sight of at least 10 more being built, with what looks like the frames of at least a dozen waiting for entry into the construction area. They appear to be a large ship that has been stripped of much of its skin, with the larger portions of many other small ships attached to them. In a parallel production line, fighters of all kinds are being assembled to supply the carriers when they are completed.`
			action
				set "EA saw the supercarriers"
			choice
				`	(Leave the planet and report back to the Navy)`
					goto leave
				`	(Investigate the tower)`
			label tower
			`	You cruise over the landscape, looking for anything interesting as you approach the tower, which on closer inspection shows it is part of a larger complex, set in a depression in the ground. Not just the roads lead here. It looks like every major system and process lies in spokes around the tower, on the side opposite you, you see a massive farming operation stretching away in a wedge till it is lost behind clouds.`
			`	The <ship> rocks from a shock of turbulence as you get close to the tower. Your radar altimeter also suddenly jumps, making the autopilot disconnect. As you take over manual control of your ship, you look down to see that the ground falls away, making a superhumanly deep shaft in which the tower resides. The tower appears to be supported underground by supports built into the black rock at the far end of the crust.`
			action
				set "EA saw the big tower"
			label leave
			`	Suddenly, a warning horn blares in your cockpit, as several of the missile batteries you experienced earlier lock on and fire. Apparently you weren't just being ignored. The missiles have apparently very crude guidance systems, since none come close this time, but you see that the top of the mysterious tower is bristling with weapons. Doors open in its sides, and a swarm of small ships pours out and attacks you. They cannot keep up with your ship as you flee in the direction you came, but the supercarriers performing a well-managed scramble takeoff in the distance will be a problem. Now would be a good time to get out and make your report.`
				launch
	npc evade
		personality launching daring ramming unconstrained vindictive
		fleet "Omega Guardian" 2
		on "scan outfits"
			set "EA scanned the supercarriers"
	on complete
		payment 1000000
		event "EA timer I"
		conversation
			`The planet seems to be in much the same condition it was when you were last here, except that the general activity level has increased. There are also more Free Worlds units working alongside the Republic. An aide appears from the ranks of maintenance crew descending on the <ship> and requests to enter the bridge.`
			`	He salutes and hands you a data pad that reads:`
			`If you're reading this, that means you made it back from your mission. Congratulations on not getting dead. It also means that I am tied up with something important enough to keep me from hearing your report, which is no doubt intriguing. I will make space tomorrow, along with a few other people who may want to hear what you have to say. Besides making sure you are in the spaceport tomorrow, that's all you have to do for now, we'll find you.`
			`		Fleet Admiral Alzaga, Combined Task Force Commander`
			``
			`You hand the data pad back to the aide, who is still standing at attention.`
			choice
				`	"Dismissed."`
					goto end
				`	"Why were you still at atttention?`
			`	The aide responds in a practiced bark that is barely lowered in volume for the fact you are indoors. "Standard customs and courtesies, sir! All personnel directed to report to a person or area will remain at attention until verbally directed otherwise, sir!"`
			choice
				`	"Very Well, Dismissed"`
			label end
			`	The aide rigidly salutes, performs and about face, and trips over the bulkhead as he leaves the bridge. He gets up quickly and leaves the ship.`
	on visit
		dialog `You must destroy or evade the supercarriers before you can complete the mission!`

mission "Endgame Alpha 4.1"
	priority
	clearance
	name "Contact the Hai"
	description "Travel to Hai-home in the Ya Hai system to make inquiries about the strange structure on Sagarra."
	source "New Argentina"
	destination "Hai-home"
	to offer
		has "event: EA timer I"
	on offer
		conversation
			`You walk into the spaceport, and make your way to the food court. You didn't receive any more instructions besides this so you sit down and wait.`
			choice
				`	(Wait.)`
					goto skipfood
				`	(Get some food.)`
			label getfood
			`	You peruse the various food vendors operating on the edges of the court, with tables in the center. In the middle, there is a temporary installation handing out military rations as well.`
			choice
				`	(Get a ration kit.)`
					goto rations
				`	(Get some real food.)`
			`	Because the planet was not heavily populated before the Alpha war, the food court (and spaceport facilities in general) are not sufficient to comfortably support the Navy operations. Hence, every food vendor has a long line of officers and enlisted personnel waiting for nourishment. The only one promising a reasonably short wait time is the store advertising "Authentic Local Cuisine".`
			`	As you approach the stall, you realize that the industry standard payment terminal attached to the ordering screen that you usually see in such areas has been removed and replaced with a simple optical scanner. A more broad inspection shows that all the terminals have been replaced.`
			choice
				`	(Make your order.)`
					goto order
				`	"Where did the payment terminals go?"`
			`	The employee replies haltingly after a pause. "No Capitan...no more tehrminals. Just scan card and we give...meal, contract will take from income later."`
			`	The employee turns to something else, stopping further communication.`
			label order
			`	You scan your Navy license and order the first thing on the menu. After a wait made much longer by the awareness of your hunger, you receive an archaic disposable food box that is so hot you nearly drop it.`
			`	Your "Ranch Special" is a large piece of meat lying on a bed of vegetables. Everything in the container is burnt so extensively that further identification is impossible.`
			choice
				`	(Eat it.)`
					goto eat
				`	(Throw it away.)`
					goto trash
				`	(Chuck it back at the cooks.)`
			label throw
			`	You pick up the meal, burning your hands again, and lob it into the food stall. The workers do not seem at all surprised by this, and only mutter about manners as they clean up the mess. A rear admiral nearby laughs uproariously. He turns to you. "Do you know, you're the fifth person I've seen do that in the past two hours. You're the highest ranking one so far though. All these d-" He snaps to attention and salutes. You turn around, see Admiral Alzaga (with Raven behind), and mirror the rear admiral's behavior. The task force commander salutes back, and says to the lower admiral, "Do you mind?"`
			`	Left alone, you sit down at your original table again.`
				goto briefing
			label eat
			`	The light plastic knife included with your meal is completely inadequate to carve the carboniferous mass. Even with your service knife, it takes several blows to break off a bite-sized peice. You bite into it, and immediately find that repetition of that action will likely result in grave personal injury. The internal qualities of the meat are eactly the same as the external, coupled with a burning heat and pain unlike any spice you've encountered before. You spit it back out, and move on to the vegetables in a faint hope something would be different. It isn't.`
			choice
				`	(Chuck it at the cooks.)`
					goto throw
				`	(Throw it away.)`
			label trash
			`	You cram the horror into an available trash receptacle (packed to bursting like the rest of the spaceport), and turn back around. Both Raven and Admiral Alzaga are seated at your table waiting for you to come back. You return to the table and sit down.`
			label rations
			`	Like everywhere else in the food court, the line is long, but thankfully it is moving much faster. After a reasonable wait, you are at the front of the line. You scan your Navy license, and receive your kit accordingly. It is tube, about the length of your forearm, marked "PERSONAL MEAL KIT, TYPE A, VARI 04" You are unsure of what this means.`
			`	At a table, you find that the tube is filled with an unmarked can, a spoon, a can opener, and a variety of small snacks. The substance in the can looks anything but appetizing, and the texture almost makes you gag, but the taste is not terrible. You finish it, idly look around the spaceport, and begin fiddling with your spoon. It is probably the strongest and most durable "disposable" utensil you've ever come across. You bend it with difficulty, and it springs out of your hand and lands underneath a nearby table.`
			choice
				`	(Leave It.)`
					goto litter
				`	(Go pick it up.)`
			`	After an embrassingly extended period of scrabbling underneath the large table, you retrieve the spoon. You look up to see Raven and Admiral Alzaga seated at your table. "You might want to keep that, <last>, it's a Navy tradition to keep your first PMK spoon, I've still got mine from basic."`
				goto ravenchoice
			label litter
			`	You go back to eating your snacks, but a moment later the errant spoon plunks back on the table, rapidly followed by Raven plunking into a seat across from you. "You know that really is quite messy to go around dropping things like that <last>. Besides, it's a tradition to keep your first PMK spoon, I've still got mine from basic." Admiral Alzaga sits down next to her.`
			label ravenchoice
			`	You take the spoon and put it into your pocket.`
			choice
				`	"Really? I didn't know you exhibited normal human sentimentality behaviors."`
					goto sentimental
				`	"Interesting, but business first."`
					goto briefing
			`	Raven stares at you dead-eyed. Very funny, "You'd be suprised how much is necessary to not be a complete outcast. Now if you're done joking, we can get to talking about the reason for this not-social call."`
				goto briefing
			label skipfood
			`	You have been waiting for an hour in the spaceport when you realize that you are in fact very, very hungry.`
			choice
				`	(Get Food.)`
					goto getfood
				`	(Wait.)`
			`	Still no sign of Admiral Alzaga...`
			choice
				`	(Wait.)`
			`	Still no sign of Admiral Alzaga......`
			choice
				`	(Wait.)`
			`	Still no sign of Admiral Alzaga.........`
			choice
				`	(Wait.)`
			`	You are startled awake by a hand vigorously slapping your back. You jump up, dimly registering Admiral Alzaga in front of you and Raven behind you, and snap to attention and offer the usual customs and courtesies. After your wits return, you see that Alzaga does not seem in the least perturbed by you being asleep.`
			`	Both of them sit down at your table and Raven begins, "You look well rested Commander. I'm glad, we have a lot to discuss."`
				goto briefing
			label briefing
			`	"The next job for you to do is a little bit different from what we've had you doing. The data you've submitted of the Alpha base is unlike any kind of organization we've come across, as you probably already figured, so we would like you to travel around and make some inquiries about it, specifically about the structure at the center of this...thing." Admiral Alzaga says.`
			choice
				"Really? That's all?"
				"Sounds simple enough."
			`	"But of course, it isn't. The kind of technology being used is almost certainly not human in origin, so your inquiries will be beyond the normal areas. You are very...well travelled, possibly the most travelled person readily available in the galaxy. Additionally, these inquiries must be made with tact, discretion, and diplomacy, as the first official contact some of these species will have will be an empire in distress. It will be important to set the right tone and ensure that even more trouble does not well up from beyond the hyperspace lanes."`
			`	Raven adds, "Please begin your inquiries with the Hai, and move through each faction from there. We'll tell you where to go for each one." She shrugs. "Should be pretty easy, honestly."`
			choice
				`	(Accept the job.)`
				`	"How do you even know where I've been?"`
					goto intel
				`	"What if something goes wrong?"`
					goto wrong
				`	"Thanks, but no thanks."`
					goto cantleave
			`	"Understood, I'll get ready to leave as soon as possible." You say, and stand to leave. Both Raven and the Admiral stand up as well, you salute, and return to your ship to begin preparations.`
				accept
			label intel
			`	Raven hoods her eyelids. "Really Commander, I am capable of finding a great many more things than I am necessarily allowed to, and besides, you signed into the government information service earlier through your ship's mainframe, so I can do whatever I want. It was in the terms you apparently didn't read."`
			`	You shrug. "Understood, I'll get ready to leave as soon as possible." You say, and stand to leave. Both Raven and the Admiral stand up as well, you salute, and return to your ship to begin preparations, looking suspiciously at your ship's computer screen.`
				accept
			label wrong
			`	Alzaga replies. "Honestly Commander, I'm not too worried about it. We'll keep sending you updates and instructions as needed to make sure the right message gets conveyed, although you might want to take a heavy ship with plenty of fuel to get out of somewhere if you need to. Besides, just one measly commander isn't much compared to what we've lost already." He winks, and stands to leave. You salute him back, and return to your ship to begin preparations.`
				accept
			label cantleave
			`	Admiral Alzaga smiles back at you. "I keep on forgetting that you are new to this whole Navy thing. You don't really get a choice. It's an order, and I even put it in writing for you. On real paper. He slides the document across to you.`
			`	"Understood, I'll get ready to leave as soon as possible." You say, and stand to leave. Both Raven and the Admiral stand up as well, you salute, and return to your ship to begin preparations.`
				accept
	on complete
		payment 50000
		conversation
			`As soon as you have stopped, a hologram message appears on your dashboard. It is Raven. "Now that you have arrived at your destination, I can now add to the orders you have already received.`
			`	"Not only shall you inquire about the structures located on Sagarra, but you must also ask if each government is willing to provide any resources to aid in the fight. Military fleets are preferred, but we will also accept humanitarian resources or assistance with housing refugees. The more the better, but please do not overstep or push in any way. The Alphas are still vevry much a human problem, and races who have been effected by them will be hostile, since it was us that created them anyway.`
			`	"Additionally, make sure that the government you are in discussion with understands that we have the capability to maintain security around assisting fleets, and that they need not fear being discovered.`
			`	"I wish you well with the Hai. They are probably our most potent allies since they are already known by many humans. Contacting higher-ups is fairly formal on Hai-home, you can file for a meeting at the spaceport embassy." The message closes out. Along with the message, there is payment of 50,000 credits.`
