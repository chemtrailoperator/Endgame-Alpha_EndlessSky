# 5.1.2 Travel from headquarters with access codes to secret system \fold
mission "Endgame Alpha 5.1.2"
	landing
	name "Black Program"
	description `Travel to the marked system, the Syndicate's black research facility.`
	source Sheratan
	destination Keyhole
	to offer
		has "Endgame Alpha 5.1.1: done"
	on offer
		event "EA Reveal Keyhole"
		conversation
			`Much like your last visit, you are vectored into the Syndicate's secure area. However, when you land, a group of people exits a ground vehicle and requests to board your ship. The group appears similar to the set of engineers from earlier, but all are wearing sunglasses, visors, or hats.`
			`	Aboard your ship, they seat themselves in your dining area and gesture for you to sit down as well. "Captain <last>, you are no doubt wondering what business the Syndicate has in your war." A tall, slender, austere looking man says.`
			choice
				`	"Fighting against the Alphas is kind of an all-of-humanity thing, so no."`
					goto allhumanity
				`	"Trying to make up for starting the last one?"`
					goto starting
				`	"Indeed. Please continue."`
			label continue
			`	"The Syndicate, as you may have guessed, is quite active in the copying of technology from beyond the limits of human space. We have produced a single weapon of immense power that will be useful to your government, and for humanity, to defeat the Alphas."`
			choice
				`	"Why just one?"`
					goto justone
				`	"Great! Where do I get it?"`
					goto researchfacility
				`	"I don't care for weapons of mass destruction, I'll be on my way."`
					goto wmdleave
			label starting
			`	One of the men at the table smirks, but the speaker does not appear to react in any way, "Captain, you must understand that although that is not the primary reason, or really one of any import, for supplying this device, it will certainly improve relations with the Republic, so yes."`
				goto continue
			label allhumanity
			`	"If you put it in those terms, you are correct. The Syndicate does not wish for the Alphas to succeed, as that would be devastating economically. What I meant was the reason for this business specifically."`
				goto continue
			label justone
			`	The austere man grows even more austere. "That information is very, very restricted. You will get more details when you reach your destination."`
			label researchfacility
			`	A man wearing very large sunglasses speaks up. "I will preface by saying that the information you are about to hear is one of the closest guarded secrets of the Syndicate. Towards the core, a nova star with nothing really around it exists. Nobody ever bothered to colonize it because of the high risk of Korath raids, but as it turned out the Korath never really go there. We've quietly put a station there for our black research. We've loaded the coordinates into your ship, and you can get more information once you get there."`
			`	The men stand up and leave your ship, and you are left to chart a course to the research facility.`
			branch hasjumpdrive
				has "outfit (flagship installed): Jump Drive"
			action
				outfit "Jump Drive" 1
			`	While you are working, a mechanic crew arrives and leaves a Jump Drive in your ship.`
			label hasjumpdrive
			``
				accept
			label wmdleave
			`	Without saying anything more, the men stand up and leave your ship, and you are left to your own devices.`
				decline

# 5.1.3 Travel from syndicate to Geminus to pick up stealth gunboat \fold
mission "Endgame Alpha 5.1.3"
	landing
	source Keyhole
	destination Geminus
	name `Weapon System Construction`
	description `Travel to <destination> for a Stealth Gunboat.`
	to offer
		has "Endgame Alpha 5.1.2: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`What you expected to be some sort of fortress is a completely normal-looking space station, albeit with advanced security systems and sensors. Overall though, it seems that the Syndicate is more focused on avoiding detetion than repelling attack. You are escorted through a variety of unmarked passages, reaching a hangar room with a large device festooned with tubes and wires sitting in the middle. Judging by the size of the support rig it is sitting on, it must weigh at least 20 tons.`
			`	"Captain! Do you like what you see?" An engineer in a lab coat says from the side of the thing.`
			label firstchoice
			choice
				`	"I don't even know what I see."`
					goto dontknow
					to display
						not "EA 5.1.3 what"
				`	"Yeah sure."`
					goto yeahsure
					to display
						not "EA 5.1.3 what"
				`	"There is no way you could launch that thing from a ship."`
					goto launchship
			label dontknow
			`	The engineer caresses a pipe lovingly. "What you're looking at is possibly the most powerful weapon ever built by mankind. It started out as a Jump Drive that we discovered had some defects. Instead of simply blinking out of existence like it usually does, the stuff inside it started resonating when we tried to open it up, thankfully we closed the first one before it got uncontrollable. The machine around the drive should contain the force being released just long enough to turn it into a devastatingly powerful blast of energy. We already tried one in deep space, and you could see the flash the size of a star back on Sundive. Of course, that's all assuming this thing work the same way..."`
			action
				set "EA 5.1.3 what"
				goto firstchoice
			label yeahsure
			`	The engineer caresses a pipe lovingly. "Don't be too close when it goes boom, just know that it will."`
			action
				set "EA 5.1.3 what"
				goto firstchoice
			label launchship
			`	He smirks. "Luckily, it won't have to, and that's where you come in. The Navy agreed to supply a special ship to deliver the weapon to Sagarra. It's got stealth technology, but that's all I know, they were as uncomfortable sharing details as we were bringing you here.`
			`	"This thing is so big that any ship firing it won't survive, so they decided to use a ship and ignore the process of launching it entirely."`
			choice
				`	"Great, where do I get one?"`
					goto getone
				`	"Why is Sagarra such a big deal?"`
			`	The engineer shrugs, "I don't know for sure why, but that's where they said it should go. I build weapons, not targets."`
			label getone
			`	"The republic said to send you to Geminus for pickup as soon as you had been briefed, so I think you should be going now." A group of uniformed agents surrounds you and escorts you back to your ship.`
				accept
	on complete
		dialog `As you land, you receive a mesage requesting you to report to the Navy special affairs office in the spaceport.`
# 5.1.4 Deliver to Syndicate \fold
mission "Endgame Alpha 5.1.4"
	priority
	clearance
	source Geminus
	destination Keyhole
	name `Weapon System Construction`
	description `Bring the specially equipped Stealth Gunboat to Keyhole in the Aurora system.`
	to offer
		has "Endgame Alpha 5.1.3: done"
	on offer
		event `EA unlock Keyhole spaceport`
		conversation
			scene `thumbnail/stealth gunboat`
			`The aide you spoke with at the navy office brings you to a small conference room, and leaves. Shortly after, another engineer-type individual enters and sits down across from you. He pushes a picture to you of a ship. it appears to be a heavily modified gunboat, with most of the external features removed.`
			choice
				`	"That's what I'm bringing to the Syndicate?"`
					goto syndicate
				`	"What's wrong with it?`
			`	"There's nothing actually wrong with it, just not a lot going on." The engineer picks up the photo again. "It's our new Stealth Gunboat, about as low-observable as it gets, and it's your job to serve it on a platter to the Syndicate. It's all ready to take off with you, Jump Drive and all."`
			choice
				`	"Great. I'll get going then."`
					accept
				`	"Why is that such a problem?"`
			`	"Tell me Captain, if you spent years working on a weapon system just to have it given to your primary competitor, how would you feel?" He starts to say more, closes it, and restarts, "But I've said enough. I wasn't supposed to talk to you much anyway." The engineer gets up and leaves without further comment.`
				accept
			label syndicate
			`	"Pretty much, unless you've got any other ideas. Look, I'm not supposed to tell you anymore than the type of ship to look for, so I'll be going now." The engineer gets up and leaves the conference room.`
				accept
	npc accompany save
		government "Republic"
		personality timid secretive escort
		ship "Stealth Gunboat (Jump)" "Pascal Delta"
	on complete
		conversation
			`The process of dropping the Pascal Delta off at its construction dock is almost instantaneous, and you are requested to go to the spaceport for further briefings.`
# 5.1.5 Take weapon system to Sagarra \fold
mission `Endgame Alpha 5.1.5`
	priority
	clearance
	source Keyhole
	destination Sagarra
	name "Payload Delivery"
	description `Escort the weapon system to <destination>, and retreat from there.`
	to offer
		has "Endgame Alpha 5.1.4: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`You approach the information desk, and present your pilot's license when it is requested by a nondescript clerk. "Good evening Captain, you have an appointment in room thirty-seven." She gestures to a door prominently marked R37 to the right of where you came in.`
			choice
				`	(Go to R37.)`
					goto R37
				`	(Deny any involvement.)`
			`	The clerk taps on her vast array of data screens for several seconds, then turns back and smiles professionally at you. "Captain, I have cancelled your appointment. Additionally, your temporary clearance to access this facility expires shortly, I must ask you to vacate the station." You are about to argue, but you see a ring of holes in the roof that look suspiciously like sniper perches. You think better of your original plan and leave without a fuss.`
				flee
			label R37
			`	As you walk over to the door, it pops open for you. Inside, it appears to be a normal size briefing room, but every wall that usually contains a screen is covered in temporary fabric. Seated at the table is yet another boring looking agent. "Captain, this is the last briefing you get before deploying the weapon system, I have instructions from the Republic, Free Worlds, and operating instructions from our designers."`
			choice
				`	"Fire away."`
			`	"First, the Republic wanted you to know that Sagarra is the highest priority target for the Weapon. Analysis of Alpha behavior suggests that the breed mutated, or was bred, to have less critical thinking and even less decision making capability. They've noticed that when communication systems are damaged on a ship, its behavior becomes erratic and almost completely undirected, indicating that the things on board are receiving quite a few instructions.`
			`	The Free Worlds said something similar, with addendum that they will wait to detonate the device as long as possible until all ships are landed on planets so that if the Alphas go crazy, they might survive. Both governments also wish me to stress that you do this as a volunteer and not under orders. Do you understand and accept these instructions?"`
			choice
				`	"Yes."`
				`	"No, I refuse."`
					goto refuse
			`	The agent pulls a recording device from his breast pocket and presses a button on the side. The light on top switches from green to red, and he replaces it. "For legal reasons I'm supposed to collect that. Now for the instructions on the weapon itself. The ship is currently being modified to accept the payload, and it is being built directly into the structure. Avoid landing as much as possible and get to Sagarra as quickly as possible, obviously, but also, protect the Weapon on the way there. What the engineers might not have told you is that we've only ever salvaged two Jump Drives with the defect that makes them useful like this.`
			`	"That's all I've been given to brief you on, and your clearance expires soon, so you'd better be leaving. Personally though, good luck." He stands up, collects a briefcase from below the table, and leaves, clapping you on the back on the way out.`
				accept
	on enter
		conversation
			`As you enter orbit in formation with the Pascal Delta, you see that the underside has been covered in signatures and messages, from the engineers, you presume. Once you leave the atmosphere, the computer system disconnects from departure control and the automatic piloting system engages, linking to your ship.`
	npc accompany save
		government "Syndicate"
		personality timid secretive escort
		ship "Stealth Gunboat (AWS)" "Weapon System"
		on destroy
			dialog `You have let the Weapon System be destroyed before reaching its target! If life were a game, you might be able to go back and start over.`
