# 5.1.2 Travel from headquarters with access codes to secret system \fold
mission "Endgame Alpha 5.1.2"
	landing
	name "Black Program"
	description `Travel to the marked system, the Syndicate's black research facility.`
	source Sheratan
	destination Keyhole
	to offer
		has "Endgame Alpha 5.1.1: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Much like your last visit, you are vectored into the Syndicate's secure area. However, when you land, a group of people exits a ground vehicle and requests to board your ship. The group appears similar to the set of engineers from earlier, but all are wearing sunglasses, visors, or hats.`
			`	Aboard your ship, they seat themselves in your dining area and gesture for you to sit down as well. "Captain <last>, you are no doubt wondering what business the Syndicate has in your war." A tall, slender, austere looking man says.`
			choice
				`	"Fighting against the Alphas is kind of an all-of-humanity thing, so no."`
					goto allhumanity
				`	"Trying to make up for starting the last one?"`
					goto starting
				`	"Indeed. Please continue."`
			label continue
			`	"The Syndicate, as you may have guessed, is quite active in the copying of technology from beyond the limits of human space. We have produced a single weapon of immense power that will be useful to your government, and for humanity, to defeat the Alphas."`
			choice
				`	"Why just one?"`
					goto justone
				`	"Great! Where do I get it?"`
					goto researchfacility
				`	"I don't care for weapons of mass destruction, I'll be on my way."`
					goto wmdleave
			label starting
			`	One of the men at the table smirks, but the speaker does not appear to react in any way, "Captain, you must understand that although that is not the primary reason, or really one of any import, for supplying this device, it will certainly improve relations with the Republic, so yes."`
				goto continue
			label allhumanity
			`	"If you put it in those terms, you are correct. The Syndicate does not wish for the Alphas to succeed, as that would be devastating economically. What I meant was the reason for this business specifically."`
				goto continue
			label justone
			`	The austere man grows even more austere. "That information is very, very restricted. You will get more details when you reach your destination."`
			label researchfacility
			`	A man wearing very large sunglasses speaks up. "I will preface by saying that the information you are about to hear is one of the closest guarded secrets of the Syndicate. Towards the core, a nova star with nothing really around it exists. Nobody ever bothered to colonize it because of the high risk of Korath raids, but as it turned out the Korath never really go there. We've quietly put a station there for our black research. The coordinates are loaded into your ship, and you can get more information once you get there."`
			branch hasjumpdrive
				has "outfit (flagship installed): Jump Drive"
			action
				outfit "Jump Drive" 1
			`	The men stand up and leave your ship, and you are left to chart a course to the research facility. While you are working, a mechanic crew arrives and leaves a Jump Drive in your ship.`
				accept
			label hasjumpdrive
			`	The men stand up and leave your ship, and you are left to chart a course to the research facility.`
				accept
			label wmdleave
			`	Without saying anything more, the men stand up and leave your ship, and you are left to your own devices.`
				decline
	on decline
		event `EA Reshroud Keyhole`
		
# 5.1.3 Travel from syndicate to Geminus to pick up stealth gunboat \fold
mission "Endgame Alpha 5.1.3"
	landing
	source Keyhole
	destination Geminus
	name `Weapon System Construction`
	description `Travel to <destination> for a Stealth Gunboat.`
	to offer
		has "Endgame Alpha 5.1.2: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`What you expected to be some sort of fortress is a completely normal-looking space station, albeit with advanced security systems and sensors. Overall though, it seems that the Syndicate is more focused on avoiding detection than repelling attack. You are escorted through a variety of unmarked passages, reaching a hangar room with a large device festooned with tubes and wires sitting in the middle. Judging by the size of the support rig it is sitting on, it must weigh at least 20 tons.`
			`	"Captain! Do you like what you see?" An engineer in a lab coat says from the side of the thing.`
			label firstchoice
			choice
				`	"I don't even know what I see."`
					goto dontknow
					to display
						not "EA 5.1.3 what"
				`	"Yeah sure."`
					goto yeahsure
					to display
						not "EA 5.1.3 what"
				`	"There is no way you could launch that thing from a ship."`
					goto launchship
			label dontknow
			`	The engineer caresses a pipe lovingly. "What you're looking at is possibly the most powerful weapon ever built by mankind. It started out as a Jump Drive that we discovered had some defects. Instead of simply blinking out of existence like it usually does, the stuff inside it started resonating when we tried to open it up, thankfully we closed the first one before it got uncontrollable. The machine around the drive should contain the force being released just long enough to turn it into a devastatingly powerful blast of energy. We already tried one in deep space, and you could see the flash the size of a star back on Sundive. Of course, that's all assuming this thing works the same way..."`
			action
				set "EA 5.1.3 what"
			goto firstchoice
			label yeahsure
			`	The engineer caresses a pipe lovingly. "Don't be too close when it goes boom, just know that it will, and it will be quite the boom."`
			action
				set "EA 5.1.3 what"
			goto firstchoice
			label launchship
			`	He smirks. "Luckily, it won't have to, and that's where you come in. The Navy agreed to supply a special ship to deliver the weapon to Sagarra. It's got stealth technology, but that's all I know, they were as uncomfortable sharing details as we were bringing you here.`
			`	"This thing is so big that any ship firing it won't survive, so they decided to install it more like an outfit on some poor ship and ignore the process of launching it entirely."`
			choice
				`	"Great, where do I get one?"`
					goto getone
				`	"Why is Sagarra such a big deal?"`
			`	The engineer shrugs, "I don't know for sure why, but that's where they said it should go. I build weapons, not targets."`
			label getone
			`	"The republic said to send you to Geminus for pickup as soon as you had been briefed, so I think you should be going now." A group of uniformed agents surrounds you and escorts you back to your ship.`
				accept

		dialog `As you land, you receive a message requesting you to report to the Navy special affairs office in the spaceport.`

			choice
				`	"Fire away."`
			`	"First, the Republic wanted you to know that Sagarra is the highest priority target for the Weapon. 			choice
				`	"Yes."`
				`	"No, I refuse."`
					goto refuse

Avoid landing as much as possible and get to Sagarra as quickly as possible, obviously, but also, protect the Weapon on the way there.
