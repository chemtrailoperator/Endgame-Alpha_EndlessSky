This is a roadmap for the Endgame Alpha storyline.

People:
[V] Raven - Navy Intelligence officer, featured heavily in storyline.
Gabriel Danforth - Son of Admiral Danforth, killed in Espionage missions by Alpha car bomb.
[V] Admiral Danforth - Commander of the Oathkeepers. 
[Free Worlds Security Advisor] - Man in charge of surveillance and intellifence in Free Worlds.
Admiral Alzaga - Navy Commander at NB New Iceland promoted to CnC of Alpha War.

Missions:

#START OF PROLOGUE - THE STORYLINE KICKS OFF (OBVIOUSLY)

setup mission "EA FW Raven Contact"
Low probability
You see Raven (Navy Intelligence) at a spaceport in FW space, but you either can't catch her or ignore her. No consequence either way.

setup mission "EA Deep Raven Contact"
Low probability
You see Raven again in the Deep, again, you can't catch her or ignore. Ignore results in defer, but no other consequences.

story mission "EA Synd Raven Contact"
Low Probability
You see Raven once more in the Syndicate. If you follow her, she tells you to take her to Geminus. If you ignore her, it defers.

story mission "EA Raven Last Contact"
Upon arriving at Geminus, Raven tells you she will be in touch. 
At this mission, a timer is started for Raven's next contact, and for a terror attack on Earth.

setup mission "EA Celebration Bombing"
A Remembrance Day celebration on Earth is bombed, ostensibly by terrorists. You are informed by a mid probability spaceport flavor mission.

story mission "EA CalltoGeminus"
Raven summons you to Geminus with the promise of work.

#END OF PROLOGUE

#START OF ESPIONAGE - YOU DO SOME INVESTIGATING AND STUFF, ALPHAS ARE DISCOVERED FOR THE FIRST TIME IN DECADES
story mission "EA operator rendesvous"
Upon arriving at Geminus, Raven tells you about the terror attacks and tells you NI wants you to rendesvous with an intelligence operator on the pirate planet Mutiny. CRITICAL

story mission "EA operator contact"
When you rendesvous with the operator on Mutiny, he wants you to escort him to Greenrock in the Shaula system.

story mission "EA Operative Death"
On Greenrock the intelligence guy goes off to do stuff, you are left to yourself with multiple opportunities to die. You find the operative tied to a car bomb. If you try to save him, you die too. Once you leave the planet, you are attacked by Alphas. Once you escape you head to Geminus to receive instructions from Raven. She takes your information and tells you to go to Danforth to tell him.

#END OF ESPIONAGE

#START OF EMERGENCE - MORE AND MORE TRACES OF THE ALPHAS ARE PRESENT, THE REPUBLIC GEARS UP FOR WAR

mission 2.0
You land at Farpoint and tell Danforth. He sends you to Bourne to get shared intelligence data codes from the FW to get the surveillance data from the Alpha fight.

mission 2.1
At Bourne you tell the FW of the situation and you get the codes to access the intelligence data, which is stored on planet. You are told to head to a Navy base on New Iceland once you have the data. When you land on New Iceland you have to go to the spaceport.

mission 2.2
You are summoned to Farpoint, ostensibly to escort Danforth and Raven back to New Iceland.

mission 2.3
You pick up Danforth and Raven on Farpoint, and see the new Supercruiser design, an upgraded cruiser with heavier weapons, armor, and two more drone bays. You are told to head to the Spaceport bar to keep doing jobs like this.

mission 2.4
Danforth tells you that you can now accept Navy Intelligence jobs via the Job Board now. Start of ~1 month timer until Alpha Emergence.

mission 2.5
You receive an emergency message from the FW (also sent to NavInt) telling you that a research vessel has gone missing in Beta Lupi, where there is also an unknown communications blackout. The ship has highly important cargo. You are sent to rescue it. It is a private ship, and the research is unknown. Discretion advised. When you find the ship and capture it you find the Alphas being researched on board revolted and killed the crew. You find a vial of green goo. [Green Goo side missions originate here]. If you explore the ship too far or incorrectly, you die. Once you land you have to head to the spaceport.

mission 2.6
You find a new department in the Free Worlds dedicated to networking with NavInt. You meet a general who tells you about the Deltas, a smarter, more human version of the Alphas that resulted from an incorrect experiment. You are given the option to turn over the green goo or keep it for yourself. No consequences either way. If you keep it, it triggers the green goo storyline. You are sent to New Iceland.

mission 2.7 and 2.8
You land at New Iceland, where the Navy base from the FW war has been reactivated to counter the new threat of Alphas. You are paid well, and told you have the opportunity to commission. If you accept, you have to head to Earth.

mission 2.9
You arrive at Earth and are given a commission and the rights to buy a uniform at the outfitter on Earth. 15 day timer to restart story.

#END OF EMERGENCE

#START OF TAKEOVER - THE ALPHAS GO ON THE OFFENSIVE AND SCORE GREAT VICTORIES, TAKING OVER MUCH TERRITORY

setup mission 3.0
A news broadcast shows you that an unknown foe has attacked the CCOR planets in the south. 

setup mission 3.1
Another news broadcast tells you that Belenos and Thuban have been conquered and Men is under attack. The takeover has been accomplished very quickly, the FW is reluctant to help out the pirate planets and the Republic has refused.

mission 3.2 and 3.3
The Republic decides to fight, and sends all non-essential or combat-ready units south to Men. That includes you. Upon arriving at Men you find a battleground. You land on Men for orders and see that Pirates are getting along just fine with everyone else. A gigantic armada is detected en route and you must fight immediately. After the fight you head to Solace to regroup.

mission 3.4
You arrive at the new joint headquarters on Solace, and are called upon for a meeting of leaders and politicians from all units. You are told the Alphas have a new giant vastly capable ship and the unprecedented assault force did not originate from a populated CCOR planet. Raven selects you to attempt penetrating the Alpha blockade to investigate Citadelle because of your experience outside human space.

mission 3.5
You slip through the blockade and discover that the Alphas did not originate from Citadelle, but there is a disturbance beyond Citadelle in what is thought to be empty space. You return to Solace with your info.

mission 3.6
You find Raven and she tells you the sensor reading you picked up are similar to those around Deneb right before the Pug invasion. There is an unknown small star system past Citadelle that was never thoroughly studied. The Alphas have huge alien drone carriers cobbled together from different races. You are sent on a patrol, ending at Pherkad.

mission 3.7
You check in on Pherkad, you are paid, and given a package to deliver to New Iceland. Kappa Centauri and Pherkad are taken over.

mission 3.8
You are sent to penetrate the blockade around Winter to rescue VIPs. You are encouraged to use your jump drive if you have it. Winter falls as you leave. Yed Prior, Alniyat, Graffias, Antares, and Han are conquered. When you return you are promoted to Captain and sent to Earth. The Alphas slow down a little.

mission 3.9 and 3.10
You are summoned to New Iceland. There, you are told to convoy a set of refugee ships to Farseer. You learn the Alphas have finished their hyperlink to an unknown system in Citadelle. They have a nearly impossible number of ships. It seems the whole of the South will fall if not more. The Navy is setting up defensive positions at chokepoints. You are told you can help the FW after this mission. The FW will be cut off from the Republic.

mission 3.12
Plans change and you are required back at New Iceland urgently. You are told that Alzaga has gone to New Argentina and you need to report to him there.

mission 3.13 and 3.14
Alzaga tells you the Alpha's organization is unusually good, and sends you on a recon mission deep in Alpha territory to investigate their suspected homeworld of Citadelle.

#END OF TAKEOVER

#START OF STALEMATE - JUST WAR

mission 4.0
You cruise around the surface of the Alpha homeworld, collecting info. You see a large alien-like tower, supercarriers being built, and are driven off by defenses. 

mission 4.1
Alzaga tells you that the tower must be alien in origin, and gives you a proposal to search throughout the galaxy with other factions to find an origin.

------------------------All previous are completed.-------------------------------------

mission 4.2
You are sent to the Hai first. The Hai can tell you nothing, but offer their assistance, understanding your position considering their own relationship with the unfettered. They dispatch a heavy battle fleet to Human space. You escort them back to New Argentina 

mission 4.3
Next, you are sent to the Remnant, who already have a huge defensive fleet. They will not give away their position yet, but they offer to outfit Human hulls with their technology.

mission 4.4
Wanderers do not know specifics, but are inclined to think it is Korath technology based on things they have seen since the Eye opened. When you return, you are introduced to the Stealth Cruiser and the Stealth Gunboat Drone.

mission 4.5
You got to the Bunrodea next, but they don't know anything or refuse to give you any information.

mission 4.6
You return to Alzaga with little to report. He asks if there are any other aliens out there. You have the option to tell him of the Gegno, the Avgi, or the Successors, or all three. He discounts the Gegno being of value because of their limited technology and distance from the Alphas, and thinks the isolation of the Successors probably means they know nothing. He sends you to gain information from the Avgi. 

mission 4.7
In talking to the Avgi, you discover that they had been contacted by the Alphas long before the research team reached them. They did not tell you this immediately because the Alphas were originally allowed to settle in the tangled shroud, but became unruly and violent, and were exterminated with thermonuclear weapons. You are given access to the world they used to be on, and see the same structure, but partially destroyed and less large and complex. You glean some information from it and decide that it is old Sestor tech.
The Avgi decline to send a battle fleet, saying that they have nothing to add but Thermonuclear weapons, but you convince them to allow a group of Republic engineers to come and be trained how to build the laser systems. (Also create gunport mounted blue laser and large lasing mediums.)
